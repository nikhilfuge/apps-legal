Printly: Receipt & Label Print — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · Last updated: 9 November 2026

Privacy at a glance: Printly works without an account. Your documents, business details, logos, images and printer settings are stored only on your device. Printly does not use advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs and does not sell your data.

1. About Printly

Printly: Receipt & Label Print (“Printly”) is an iPhone and iPad app published by Neel Studios for creating receipts, invoices, labels and other documents and printing them on Bluetooth thermal receipt printers.

2. Information stored on your device

Documents you create (including items, prices, customer details and notes), business profiles (such as business name, address, phone, email, tax registration numbers and logo), templates, imported images and PDFs, printer settings and app preferences are saved in Printly's private storage on your device.

Printly keeps a print history so you can see recent print jobs. It records the document title, printer name, time and result. It does not store the content of printed documents.

This information is not uploaded to a Printly server. If you use iCloud or device backups, it may be included in your device backups under Apple's terms.

3. Bluetooth and printing

Printly uses Bluetooth only when you search for or connect to a printer, and only to discover nearby printers and send your print jobs to them. iOS asks for your permission the first time. Printly stores the identifier and name of printers you add so it can reconnect to them. Print data is sent directly from your device to your printer.

4. Camera, photos and receipt scanning

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a paper receipt. Photos and files are accessed only when you choose them through Apple's photo and file pickers, for example to import a logo, an image or a PDF to print.

Text recognition for scanned receipts runs on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Scanned images and recognised text are not uploaded. You review and edit every recognised value before it is saved.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Printly Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. Printly does not receive your payment card details.

Printly uses RevenueCat, Inc. to validate purchases and manage access to Printly Pro. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated by the app, your purchase history for Printly, and technical information needed to validate App Store transactions (such as product identifiers, app version and device type). No name, email address, document content or business details are shared with RevenueCat. This information is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

6. AI drafting

The current version of Printly does not send any information to an AI service. If a future version offers AI-assisted drafting, it will send only the description you type, only when you choose to create a draft, and this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

7. Data we do not collect

Printly does not require an account and does not collect your documents, business details, customer details, images, scanned receipts, printer data or location. Printly does not include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

8. Data retention and deletion

Information stored on your device stays there until you delete it. You can delete individual documents, profiles, templates and printers in the app, erase everything in Settings › Privacy & data › Erase all data, or delete the app. You can also export your data as a JSON file from the same screen.

Printly Pro can export your receipts and invoices for a chosen year as PDF and CSV files. These files are created on your device and only leave it when you choose where to send or save them, for example by email or to Files.

Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat according to their own retention policies so that your purchases can be restored.

9. Children's privacy

Printly is a business productivity app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your choices and rights

You control Printly's access to Bluetooth, the camera, photos and files through iOS permissions and can change them at any time in iOS Settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Because Printly keeps your content on your device and does not maintain a server-side copy, there is generally no such data for us to provide or delete. For purchase records, contact us and we will help with requests that involve RevenueCat.

11. Third-party services

Printly relies on Apple for app distribution and payments and on RevenueCat for purchase validation and entitlement management. These services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Security

Printly stores your data in its private app storage with iOS data protection. The app is designed not to log document content, business details or images.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Printly's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning Printly, please email neelstudios2019@gmail.com or use the support contact published with the Printly App Store listing.
