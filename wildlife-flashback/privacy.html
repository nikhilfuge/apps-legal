Wildlife Flashback Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

Privacy at a glance: Wildlife Flashback works entirely on your Mac. It collects no data, makes no network connections and never uploads your photographs.

1. About Wildlife Flashback

Wildlife Flashback is a macOS application that helps you review, tag and organise wildlife photographs from your trips, find duplicates and soft-focus frames, and create expedition reports.

2. Data we do not collect

Wildlife Flashback does not collect, store or receive any of your data. It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising, crash-reporting services or cloud processing.

The app does not have permission to make network connections, so macOS prevents it from sending anything over the internet.

3. Photographs and folders

Wildlife Flashback reads only the folders and files you choose. Your photographs stay where you keep them. The app never uploads them, and it never permanently deletes them: files you choose to clean up are moved to a holding folder or to the Trash, and file moves can be undone.

4. Data stored on your Mac

To do its work, the app keeps the following in its own container on your Mac. None of it is sent to us or anyone else.

  • A catalogue of file locations, photo metadata (such as capture date, camera details and GPS location), your review decisions, species tags and file-organisation history.
  • Thumbnails, with metadata removed.
  • Your settings.
  • An identification model, only if you choose to install one. No model is included, and the app never downloads one. An installed model runs on your Mac.

5. Purchases

Wildlife Flashback Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether Pro is unlocked. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Please refer to Apple's privacy documentation for how Apple handles purchases.

6. Files you export

Reports, checklists and photo collections you export are saved to a location you choose. They leave your Mac only if you share them yourself.

7. Links to websites

Some buttons in the app, such as Browse Core ML Models, open a web page in your default browser. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies.

8. Data retention and removal

Data stays on your Mac until you remove it. To remove everything the app has stored, quit the app and delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.neelstudios.WildlifeFlashback. Your original photographs are not affected.

9. Children's privacy

Wildlife Flashback is not directed specifically at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Your choices and rights

You control which folders Wildlife Flashback can read. Because we hold no copy of your photographs or other data, there is no data for us to provide, correct or delete.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Wildlife Flashback's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

12. Contact

For privacy questions concerning Wildlife Flashback, please email neelstudios2019@gmail.com or use the support contact published with the Wildlife Flashback App Store listing.
